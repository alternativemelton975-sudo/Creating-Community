The Silent Watcher

In shadows deep, the raven flies,
A silent watcher of the skies.
With eyes that gleam in darkest night,
It guards the secrets out of sight.

Wings of Freedom

Upon the wind, the raven soars,
Breaking free from earthly shores.
With wings that span both time and space,
It finds its home in endless grace.

Echoes of the Past

Through ancient woods and forgotten lands,
The raven calls with haunting hands.
Its voice a bridge to days gone by,
A link between the earth and sky.

Shadow's Embrace

In twilight's hush, the raven glides,
Embraced by shadows, where mystery hides.
A guardian of secrets, both old and new,
It weaves the night with a spectral hue.

Flight of the Ancients

Across the ages, the raven flies,
Bearing tales beneath ancient skies.
A messenger of wisdom, dark and bright,
It soars through time, a timeless flight.

Keeper of Secrets

With feathers dark as midnight's veil,
